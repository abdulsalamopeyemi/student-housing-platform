## Writing rules

- Direct and trustworthy.
- Use sentence case.
- Never hide mandatory fees.
- Do not claim “100% safe”, “scam-free”, “official”, or “verified” without evidence and a defined process.
- Prefer outcome-specific labels such as “Request inspection” instead of generic “Submit”.
